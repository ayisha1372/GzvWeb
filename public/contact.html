How to Reach Us

Getting Here

We are located in the heart of Tirur, easily accessible by road, rail, and bus.

Address

Fathima Zahra Islamic Women's College
Tirur, Malappuram District
Kerala — 676 101

By Road

Accessible via NH-66. Tirur town is well-connected by KSRTC and private buses from Calicut, Malappuram, and Thrissur.

Nearest Railway Station

Tirur Railway Station — approx. 2 km from campus. Auto-rickshaws and cabs readily available.

Nearest Airport

Calicut International Airport (CCJ) — approx. 35 km. Cab services available to campus.
